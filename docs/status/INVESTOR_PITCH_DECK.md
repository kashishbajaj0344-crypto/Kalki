## APPENDIX: FAQs

### Q: Isn't giving unlicensed advice illegal?

**A**: We NEVER tell users to skip licensed professionals when required. Kalki explicitly states "Hire licensed structural engineer" for load-bearing calculations, "Hire licensed electrician" for electrical panels, etc. We're a guidance tool, not a replacement for licensed professionals.

### Q: What if AI gives wrong advice and someone gets hurt?

**A**: 
1. All guidance reviewed by licensed professionals before going live
2. LLM validation layer (2-stage verification)
3. Extensive liability insurance ($5M policy)
4. Terms of service: User assumes responsibility
5. Safety warnings on every step

### Q: Can't users just Google this?

**A**: Yes, but they'll spend 100+ hours researching, still make mistakes, and have no personalized guidance. Kalki is like ChatGPT for construction - aggregates all knowledge and provides context-aware answers instantly.

### Q: Why won't Autodesk or Procore just build this?

**A**: 
1. They're focused on professional users (not DIY)

3. Innovator's dilemma (cannibalizes existing revenue)

### Q: What about liability/insurance?

**A**: 
1. E&O insurance ($5M coverage)
2. Terms of service (user assumes risk)
3. Licensed professional requirements (for structural/engineering)
4. Industry standard disclaimers

### Q: How do you scale customer support?
